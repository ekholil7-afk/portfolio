ABOUT ME
Ebrahim Kholil
Student & Graphic Designer · Gazipur, Bangladesh

I'm Ebrahim Kholil, a student and graphic designer based in Gazipur, Bangladesh. With a strong passion for creativity and continuous learning, I enjoy exploring the intersection of design, technology, and visual storytelling.

My interests span across Graphic Design, Web Design, UI/UX Design, VFX, CGI, and Animation. I am constantly seeking new knowledge and opportunities to expand my skill set, believing that growth comes from curiosity, dedication, and a willingness to learn.

As a designer, I focus on creating visually engaging and meaningful work — with experience in vector design, poster design, banner design, logo design, and sketching. Outside of design, I enjoy drawing, gaming, watching anime, traveling, and singing.
